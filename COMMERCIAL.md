# Commercial licensing

A commercial license may be negotiated for Monutchee-owned material covered by
the dual-license policy, for example when a distributor needs permission to keep
modifications to covered code proprietary instead of satisfying GPLv3.

Business internal use already permitted by GPLv3 does not require a commercial
purchase. Commercial distribution is also allowed under GPLv3 when its terms
are satisfied.

Contact the maintainers through the contact options on
https://github.com/Monutchee to request an agreement. Identify the repository,
version, intended distribution and rights needed; do not post confidential
business details in public issues.

This document is an invitation to negotiate, not a commercial license grant.
Alternative permissions apply only after a written agreement is executed by
the relevant rights holder. Scope, fees, support, warranties and other terms
must be specified in that agreement. Until then, the applicable open-source
licenses govern. Third-party terms remain applicable under either option.
